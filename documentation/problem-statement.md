# Problem Statement

## Background

Hostels consume a large amount of water every day for activities such as bathing, washing, cleaning, and drinking. However, water usage is often not continuously monitored.

## Problems Identified

- Taps may be left running unnecessarily.
- Overhead tanks may overflow.
- Excessive water consumption may go unnoticed.
- Water leakage or abnormal usage may not be identified quickly.
- Hostel management may not have real-time usage information.

## Impact

These problems can lead to unnecessary water wastage, increased resource consumption, and difficulty in managing hostel water efficiently.

## Need for the Solution

A smart monitoring system is needed to continuously track water usage, identify unusual consumption patterns, and provide timely alerts to help reduce water wastage.

## Proposed Direction

The proposed AI-Based Smart Hostel Water Usage Monitor combines IoT-based monitoring with AI-based analysis to support smarter and more efficient hostel water management.

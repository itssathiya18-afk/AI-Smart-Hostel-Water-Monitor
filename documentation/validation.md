# Prototype Validation

## Objective

The prototype is designed to evaluate whether a smart water-monitoring dashboard can help hostel users understand water consumption and identify possible wastage.

## Validation Criteria

The prototype focuses on:

- Ease of understanding
- Usefulness of water usage information
- Clarity of AI alerts
- Usefulness of recommendations
- Potential to reduce water wastage

## Prototype Improvements

Based on the initial feedback, the improved prototype includes:

- Real-time alert concepts
- Block-level monitoring
- Usage history
- AI-generated recommendations
- Feedback collection

## Expected Outcome

The improved prototype is expected to make water usage easier to monitor and help users identify unusual consumption more quickly.

## Next Validation Step

The prototype will be tested with hostel students to collect actual user feedback. Their responses will be used to further refine the dashboard and its features.
